About AlphaCouncil

Understanding GCC markets, one portfolio at a time

AlphaCouncil turns the noise of Gulf equity markets into clear, structured, defensible intelligence — so serious investors can decide with confidence, not guesswork.

Our Mission

To give every GCC investor the analytical depth of an institutional research desk — grounded in real data, explained in plain language, and honest about what it does and doesn’t know. We don’t tell you what to buy. We help you understand what you own.

Why we exist

Built for the Gulf, not adapted to it

Global tools treat the GCC as an afterthought. We started from the region’s realities — its sectors, disclosure norms, and dividend culture — and built up.

GCC-native

Coverage across Saudi (Tadawul), UAE (ADX / DFM), Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain — calibrated to regional sector dynamics, oil-cycle sensitivity, and the income focus that defines Gulf portfolios.

AI-powered

A multi-model AI Analyst Council and a 12-dimension scoring engine read the same fundamentals, peers and news a professional would — then surface the tensions instead of hiding them.

For serious investors

No hype, no signals-for-clicks. Structured analysis you can interrogate, sourced to the data behind it, designed for people who hold real positions and want to understand them.

Our philosophy

Principles we don’t bend

01

Data-driven

Every score and statement traces back to a real data point — scraped fundamentals, price history, disclosures or news. If we don’t have the data, we say so and show a dash, never a guess.

02

Transparent

We show our methodology, cite our sources, and surface the limitations. Confidence is labelled. Conflicting signals are presented, not smoothed over.

03

Educational

Our job is to help you understand your holdings and the forces acting on them — to make you a better-informed investor, not a more dependent one.

04

Never licensed advice

AlphaCouncil provides information and analysis. It is not a licensed financial adviser, does not manage money, and never issues personal buy or sell directives. The decisions remain yours.
