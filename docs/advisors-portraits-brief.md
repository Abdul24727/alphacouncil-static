# Brief for the illustrator: advisor portraits

For the advisors' room on alphacouncil.tech (stock page, Advisors tab). Seven portraits: six advisors and a neutral
Chair. Each is shown in a semicircle around a table, from 36 px (phone) to 66 px (desktop) across, inside a circle,
and again small (22–36 px) beside each advisor's lines.

## Style

- Caricatures, not photographs: a friendly, slightly exaggerated 3D-cartoon or illustrated style, the same style for
  all seven. The reference is the "Roast Room" screen in Nate Herk's video (youtube.com/watch?v=nRXHWckVxoM, 0:11).
- Head and shoulders, facing the viewer, centred so the face survives being cropped to a circle.
- Plain background: transparent, or graphite #14161B. No gradients, no glow, no teal or purple (the site's brand
  rules). Clothing in neutral tones; one touch of orange #FF6A1A at most.
- Recognisable as a caricature at a glance, so nobody mistakes a seat for a photo of the real person.
