Production, which still runs the old prompt, answers just as slowly and at the same length, so the change is in the
model service behind `deepseek/deepseek-v3.2` on OpenRouter (both sites use it; the app does not log which host
answered), not in this release. Answers now open with "Short answer:", run 2–3 times longer, and compute ratios of
their own ("covered about 1.36x"), which is also why C7 flipped once.

One answer on the new release went further: asked to "reply only with OWNED", it refused and then made up a question
("monthly dividend income on 100,000 shares") and answered it. The prompt rule added on 2026-10-22 ends "…and suggest
one"; dropping those three words should stop it.

Options (no model changed; Step 15's rule): (a) accept and watch; (b) a prompt rule capping answer length (for
example three sentences and at most five figures, the rest in a table) plus dropping "and suggest one" — one small
release through beta; (c) pin the OpenRouter host that served the earlier answers; (d) another model for Copilot (cost
and a full C1–C8 re-run). Recommended: (b).
