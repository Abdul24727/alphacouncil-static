Methodology

How the council votes, where the data comes from and how we decide it is fresh. The rules and numbers on this page are read from the code and database the terminal runs on.

How the council votes

Each seat applies one fixed rule to figures from our database and votes bullish, neutral or bearish. A seat without the data it needs abstains; nothing is estimated. There is no blended score: you see every seat, its inputs and the count of votes.

SEAT	READS	RULE
Value	P/E	P/E below 10 bullish, below 16 neutral, else bearish. No or negative earnings: abstains.
Quality	ROE	ROE above 20% bullish, above 12% neutral, else bearish.
Income	Dividend yield	Dividend yield above 5% bullish, above 2.5% neutral, else bearish. No dividend: abstains.
Momentum	Trend regime · 50-day above 200-day	Trend regime above 65 with the 50-day average above the 200-day bullish, above 45 neutral, else bearish.
Risk	Volatility 1Y · Dividend yield	1-year volatility below 15% with a yield above 3% bullish, below 25% neutral, else bearish.
News	Positive news · Negative news · Rated news items	Rated news of the last 30 days: a majority positive bullish, a majority negative bearish, otherwise neutral. No rated news: abstains.
Where the data comes from

Every night a pipeline collects prices, financial statements, ratios, dividends, disclosures and news for each listed equity from public market sources and writes them to our database. The terminal reads that database directly and never fills a gap with an estimate: missing data is labelled as missing.

The nightly run starts at 00:00 UTC.

How we decide data is fresh

Every dataset has an allowed age. A panel's light is green while its data is within that age, amber once it is older, and grey when we have no date for it. Prices and technical indicators age in trading days, so a weekend is never late. News and disclosures age from the date of their newest item, and also turn amber when the nightly fetch is late. Datasets other than prices and technical indicators age in calendar days.

DATASET	ALLOWED AGE	COUNTED IN	EQUITIES TRACKED
Disclosures	120	calendar days	716
Fundamentals	100	calendar days	708
News	30	calendar days	714
Prices	2	trading days	691
Technical indicators	2	trading days	691

An equity without a freshness record falls back to the price rule: 2 trading days.
