How it works
BRIEF
The Chair restates your question. If it asks the room to choose, it frames two to four readings of the data — never actions.
ANSWERS
Each advisor writes their own full answer at the same time, from their own principles and from our data alone.
FAULT LINES
The Chair names where they disagree and the figures it turns on.
CHALLENGES
Each advisor challenges, by name, the answer they disagree with most, and may concede a point.
FINAL WORD
Each gives a final position in two or three sentences: what changed, and what data would change it.
THE ANSWER
The Chair writes the room's complete answer: where it agrees, where it splits, and what the data cannot tell. Never buy, sell or hold.

Every figure comes from AlphaCouncil's database and carries the tag of the dataset it was read from. Figures the checks cannot find in the data are marked.

KEY POINTS

Key points appear here as each step lands.
