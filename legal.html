Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information AlphaCouncil collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We aim to collect only what we need to run the Service well.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and authentication details when you register.
  • Portfolio & preferences — the holdings and risk profile you choose to add, used to personalise your dashboard and analysis.
  • Usage data — basic technical and interaction data to keep the Service secure and improve it.

2. How we use it

We use your information to provide and personalise the Service, to authenticate you, to respond to enquiries, and to maintain security and reliability. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Your portfolio data

Your holdings are yours. We use them only to power your own dashboard, adviser and Council reviews. We do not sell, share or publish your portfolio, and we do not compile it with other users’ data for resale.

4. Cookies

AlphaCouncil uses only strictly-essential cookies — the ones needed to sign you in and keep the site secure. We do not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking cookies, so no cookie-consent banner is required. Everything we store is listed below.

  • ac_token (cookie) — Essential, authentication. Keeps you signed in after login; it is HttpOnly and Secure so scripts cannot read it. A session cookie by default (cleared when you close the browser), or kept up to 30 days if you choose “Remember me”. First-party.
  • ac_csrf (cookie) — Essential, security. Protects against cross-site request forgery (CSRF). Set alongside your session and removed when you sign out. First-party.
  • ac.lang (browser local storage, not a cookie) — Functional, preference. Remembers whether you chose English or Arabic. It stays until you clear your browser storage and is not a tracking identifier. First-party.

Two third-party services receive your IP address so they can work, but they set no cookies on this site: Cloudflare Turnstile (bot-and-abuse protection, shown only on the sign-up page) and Google Fonts (typography).

5. Data security & retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted credentials and access controls. We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your account data, and you can contact us to exercise privacy rights available under applicable law. To request deletion of your account, contact us.
