Meet your AI Analyst Council.

Some questions need more than one perspective. For deeper portfolio reviews, AlphaCouncil uses specialized AI analyst members to challenge the thesis, compare risks, and produce one clear synthesis.

Risk Analyst

Reviews concentration, volatility, downside exposure, and whether portfolio risk matches your tolerance.

Valuation Analyst

Checks valuation, sector comparison, historical multiples, and whether stocks look expensive or reasonable.

Income Analyst

Reviews dividends, income dependency, payout behavior, and sustainability signals.

Momentum Analyst

News Analyst

Macro Analyst

Multiple perspectives. One clear synthesis.

Instead of relying on a single AI response, your AI Analyst Council cross-checks your portfolio thesis. Each analyst member reviews the data from a different angle, helping you identify risks, opportunities, and blind spots.
